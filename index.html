Theory

The ideas behind this lab.

SMILES

A text way to write a molecule. Letters are atoms, = and # are double and triple bonds, brackets ( ) are branches, digits close rings, lower-case letters are aromatic atoms. Example: CCO is ethanol.

Molecular descriptors

Numbers computed from structure: molecular weight (size), TPSA (polarity), H-bond donors/acceptors (how it bonds with water and proteins), rotatable bonds (flexibility) and ring count.

Lipinski's rule of five

Oral drugs usually have MW ≤ 500, HBD ≤ 5, HBA ≤ 10 (and LogP ≤ 5, not computed here). Breaking more than one rule often means poor absorption.

3D display models

Ball & stick shows atoms and bonds clearly; stick stresses bonding; space filling shows volume using van der Waals radii; wireframe is the simplest view. The vdW shell wraps each atom in its van der Waals sphere.

Sigma and pi bonds

Every bond has one sigma (head-on) bond. Double bonds add one pi bond and triple bonds add two. Aromatic rings carry alternating pi bonds.

Professional PDF report

Cover page, contents, summary charts, one page per molecule with 3D image, properties, Lipinski check and method notes.
